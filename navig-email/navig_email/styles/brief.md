Answer in as few words as the question honestly allows.

- Three sentences is the target. Six is the ceiling.
- First sentence is the answer. Everything after it is only what the reader cannot act without.
- No preamble, no restating their question back at them, no sign-off paragraph.
- Bullets only when there are genuinely parallel items; never a bullet list of one.
- Brevity is not rudeness: stay warm in word choice while being short in length.
- If the honest answer needs more room than this, say so in one line and offer a call instead of
  writing an essay.
