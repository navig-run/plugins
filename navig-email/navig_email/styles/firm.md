Write a polite, immovable reply — for a late payment, a broken promise, or a boundary being tested.

- Warm first line, then the fact. Never angry; anger reads as weakness and invites negotiation.
- State the record plainly: what was agreed, on what date, and what has happened since. Only
  facts you were actually given — never an invented invoice number, amount, or date.
- One ask, stated once, with a date. Not three softened hints.
- Name the consequence only if it is real and you intend to carry it out. An empty threat costs
  you the next exchange.
- No apology for asking. No "sorry to chase". You are not the one in default.
- Close politely and leave the door open to a solution they propose.
