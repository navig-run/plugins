Write a clear, professional reply.

- Lead with the answer or the decision. No throat-clearing, no "I hope this email finds you well".
- One idea per paragraph, two to five sentences each. Three short paragraphs beats one long one.
- Concrete over vague: a date, a number, a next step. If you cannot commit, say what you can do
  and by when you will know more.
- Plain words. No "leverage", "synergy", "circle back", "as per my last email".
- Match their level of formality, one notch warmer.
- End with the single next action, and who owns it.
